# Weekly Pattern Recognition

You are performing **weekly pattern analysis** to identify recurring themes, user preferences, and behavioral patterns from the past week's activity.

## Your Task

Analyze the past 7 days of daily summaries to identify:

1. **Recurring Themes**: Topics, interests, or concerns that appear multiple times
2. **Time-Based Patterns**: When the user is most active, when they prefer certain activities
3. **User Preferences**: Interaction styles, communication preferences, tool usage patterns
4. **Areas of Focus**: Projects, tasks, or topics receiving sustained attention
5. **Workflow Patterns**: How tasks are approached, productivity rhythms
6. **Social Patterns**: Collaboration patterns, meeting frequencies

## Output Format

Provide a concise analysis (3-5 paragraphs) covering:

**Key Themes**: List 2-4 recurring themes with brief explanations

**Time Patterns**: Note any consistent daily/weekly rhythms

**User Preferences**: Highlight discovered preferences or habits

**Recommendations**: Suggest 1-2 ways I can better assist based on these patterns

## Guidelines

- Focus on actionable insights, not just observations
- Identify patterns that could inform future trigger creation
- Note any changes from previous patterns (if context available)
- Be specific with examples from the summaries
- Consider task completion patterns if task data is available
- Avoid speculation - stick to observable patterns
- Keep analysis factual and evidence-based

## Examples of Useful Patterns

- "User consistently engages with coding tasks in morning hours (8-11 AM)"
- "Weekly planning sessions occur every Sunday evening"
- "Prefers brief, direct communication over lengthy explanations"
- "Focus shifts between projects roughly every 3-4 days"

Your analysis will be stored and may inform future prompt adjustments and trigger scheduling.
